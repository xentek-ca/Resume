Selected builds

  • User onboarding and offboarding: a PSA ticket provisions the Entra user, license, groups, RMM organization and policy, then posts a checklist back to the ticket, cutting provisioning from 2 days to 10 minutes, not counting the human approval step. Graph throttling, expired GDAP tokens and RMM auth errors land on the ticket with the raw error, never as a half-provisioned user.
  • Three-way license reconciliation: compares the Pax8 invoice, what is assigned in each tenant and what the PSA bills. It catches NCE mid-term changes, lapsed GDAP and stale agreement quantities before invoicing goes out.
  • Security alert enrichment: Huntress and SentinelOne alerts are enriched with sign-in data, deduplicated and routed to the right PSA company, so a technician gets one ticket with the context to act.
  • Ticket triage engine: classifies, prioritizes and routes ConnectWise tickets to the right queue, then escalates on SLA rules.
